OBJECTIVE
  Populate docs/01 ADR/ from empty, so that every decision the BRD and PRD will need either
  has an APPROVED record or is an OPEN row with a named owner. Nothing after this goal may
  make a decision that should have been an ADR.

CONTEXT - read before starting
  CLAUDE.md . CONSTITUTION.md (Art III governs this goal entirely) . GOTCHAS.md
  docs/00 SourceOfTruth/  SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-003 - all three, in full

  Binding rules:
  - The ledger starts EMPTY. Every record is new. ADR ids are FR-ADR-nnn, allocated in filing
    order, never reused.
  - FAIL-CLOSED: every record is created NOT_DECIDED + HOLD_PENDING_DECISION + NOT_RELEASED.
    A record only moves to APPROVED with a decided_by and a decision_date.
  - Art III.9: the build session rules non-reserved TECHNICAL questions and logs each as one
    [JUDGMENT CALL] line. Reserved matters (money, irreversible, secrets, legal, scope, waiving
    a quality check) are NOT ruled here even where the answer looks obvious.
  - Adopting a STRICTER option is not a waiver and needs no signature (Art III.10). That is how
    the correctness floors in Art IV can be ruled by the build session.
  - Decisions already taken in conversation and not yet filed MUST get a record: the simulated
    payment provider (Art V.5), the SRC-002/SRC-003 numbering divergence from the prior project,
    and the read-only AWS credential grant (Art VIII.2).

  OUT OF SCOPE: writing the BRD. Deciding anything reserved. Any code.

METHOD - the five-lens sweep, one agent per lens, run in parallel
  Each lens reads all three frozen sources independently and lists every decision that is
  UNDETERMINED under its lens. Lenses:
    1. Data shape        entities, keys, constraints, what is nullable, what cascades
    2. Concurrency       every place two actors touch one thing - this is the product's core
    3. Money and time    currency, tax, refunds, timezones, showtime boundaries, retention
    4. User-visible      what a person sees, is told, and is stopped from doing
    5. Operations        config, health, shutdown, logging, queues, deploys, failure modes
  Then a merge pass hunts contradictions BETWEEN lenses. Two lenses that resolved the same
  question differently is the defect class this method exists to catch.

DELIVERABLES
  1. One FR-ADR-nnn.md per decision, each carrying: context, the decision, the alternatives
     rejected and why, the source clause it derives from, and its consequences.
  2. ADR_DECISION_RELEASE_LEDGER.tsv with these columns, header-anchored, never position-anchored:
     ledger_id  pack_id  adr_id  title  decision_type  decision_owner  decision_status
     decision_outcome  release_decision  release_status  decided_by  decision_date
     source_file  evidence_record  last_updated  notes  included_in_brd  included_in_prd
     included_in_bible  included_in_datamodel
  3. Every included_in_* cell starts NOT_INCLUDED. That is CORRECT - the derived documents do
     not exist yet.
  4. A README.md defining every column and every legal value.

DEFINITION OF DONE - binary
  [ ] every decision found by the five-lens sweep is either an ADR or an Art X row. Print the
      split: N ruled, M raised. Neither number may be asserted without the list behind it.
  [ ] zero ADRs carry decision_status APPROVED without both decided_by and decision_date
  [ ] zero RESERVED-class decisions were ruled by the build session. List the reserved ones and
      who owns each.
  [ ] every ADR's source_file cites a clause that exists in SRC-001, SRC-002 or SRC-003.
      Run the citation checker; zero dangling.
  [ ] the merge pass reports every cross-lens contradiction it found, or explains why zero is
      a believable result. Zero is a suspicious answer, not a good one.
  [ ] every included_in_* cell reads NOT_INCLUDED
  [ ] PROGRESS.md appended; a handoff is written by /tt

GUARDRAILS
  - Silence never means approval. A record left undecided stays NOT_DECIDED.
  - Credentials never appear in an ADR or the ledger.
  - An unknown becomes an Art X row, never a plausible guess.
  - Create nothing in AWS.
