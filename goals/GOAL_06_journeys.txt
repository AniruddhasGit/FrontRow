OBJECTIVE
  Write docs/11 Journeys/ - persona journeys that walk the product end to end and find what a
  per-screen specification cannot see: the SEAMS between screens.

  On this product the seams are where the money is. A hold expiring while the customer is on the
  payment screen is not a bug in the seat map and not a bug in checkout. It lives between them,
  which is why no per-screen spec catches it.

  Binding rules:
  - A journey is a WALK, not a feature list. Each step names the surface, the state the user is
    in, what they do, and what they leave with.
  - EVERY step names the seam it crosses: a page boundary, a process boundary (API to worker),
    a system boundary (application to Redis, to Postgres, to TMDB, to the payment provider), or
    a time boundary (a TTL, a sales cut-off, a cancellation window).
  - A defect found here is a defect IN THE PRD, and it is filed as such - not fixed silently in
    the journey document.
  - "Found zero defects" is a SUSPICIOUS result requiring an explanation, not a good one. If a
    lane reports zero, it re-walks with a different starting state before that result stands.

  OUT OF SCOPE: changing the PRD in this goal. File the defect; the fix is its own unit of work.

DELIVERABLES - the walks that must exist
  Customer  : first visit to ticket in hand . returning customer books again . cancels a booking
              . a seat is taken while they were choosing . the hold expires ON the payment screen
              . payment fails and they retry . they double-click confirm . they close the tab
              mid-checkout and come back
  Guest     : browses without an account, then tries to book (OPEN-BOOK-02 decides what happens)
  Staff     : validates a ticket at the door . a ticket already used . a ticket for another
              cinema . a ticket for a show that already started . the internet drops
              (OPEN-TICKET-02)
  Manager   : creates a showtime . sets pricing . reads occupancy for their own cinema . tries to
              read another cinema's occupancy and is refused
  Admin     : publishes an imported movie . the sync ran and TMDB was down . an imported field
              was edited by a human and the sync runs again (OPEN-TMDB-04)

DEFINITION OF DONE - binary
  [ ] every P-row in the PRD is touched by at least one journey step, OR is explicitly listed as
      untouched with the reason. Print both counts.
  [ ] every journey step names its seam. Zero steps with an empty seam field.
  [ ] DEFECTS_FOUND.tsv has one row per defect, each naming the PRD section it lands against and
      whether it is a gap, a contradiction, or an unhandled edge
  [ ] zero lanes report zero defects without a written explanation of why zero is believable
  [ ] the four time-boundary walks exist and each states the exact user-visible outcome: hold
      expires on the payment screen, sales cut-off passes mid-checkout, cancellation window
      closes while the cancel page is open, TMDB sync runs mid-session
  [ ] the consistency check exits 0
  [ ] PROGRESS.md appended; a handoff is written by /tt
