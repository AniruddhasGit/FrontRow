OBJECTIVE
  Write docs/11 Journeys/ - persona journeys that walk the product end to end and find what a
  per-screen specification cannot see: the SEAMS between screens.

  On this product the seams are where the money is. A hold expiring while the customer is on the
  payment screen is not a bug in the seat map and not a bug in checkout. It lives between them,
  which is why no per-screen spec catches it.

  Binding rules:
  - A journey is a WALK, not a feature list. Each step names the surface, the state the user is
    in, what they do, and what they leave with.
  - EVERY step names the seam it crosses: a page boundary, a process boundary (API to worker),
    a system boundary (application to Redis, to Postgres, to S3, to the queue, to SES, to the
    simulated payment provider), or a time boundary (a TTL, a sales cut-off, a display window).
  - A defect found here is a defect IN THE PRD, and it is filed as such - not fixed silently in
    the journey document.
  - "Found zero defects" is a SUSPICIOUS result requiring an explanation, not a good one. If a
    lane reports zero, it re-walks with a different starting state before that result stands.

  OUT OF SCOPE: changing the PRD in this goal. File the defect; the fix is its own unit of work.

DELIVERABLES - the walks that must exist (revised by SRC-003)
  Customer  : first visit to receipt in hand . returning customer books again . a seat is taken
              while they were choosing . THE 3-MINUTE HOLD EXPIRES ON THE PAYMENT SCREEN .
              simulated payment fails and they retry . they double-click confirm . they close the
              tab mid-checkout and come back . they try to book an 11th seat . SES FAILS AFTER
              PAYMENT IS CAPTURED and they have no receipt (OPEN-EMAILFAIL-01 - walk it, do not
              invent the policy)
  Guest     : browses without an account, then tries to book and is required to register
              (SRC-003 OPEN-BOOK-02) - and the walk must say what happens to their seat selection
              across the registration detour. That seam is where a customer is lost.
  Entry     : the customer arrives with the emailed reference and a human checks it . the same
              reference is presented twice (nothing prevents it - SRC-003 OPEN-TICKET-02 accepts
              this; the walk records what the human actually does)
  Manager   : reads occupancy for an assigned hall . tries to read a hall they are NOT assigned to
              and is refused . tries any write at all and is refused (managers are read-only)
  Admin     : creates a movie by hand and uploads a poster . sets a display window and it expires .
              creates a showtime and prices it . withdraws a showtime that already has bookings
              (OPEN-SHOWCANCEL-01 is OPEN - walk it and file what the documents cannot answer)
