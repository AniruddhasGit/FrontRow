  Binding rules:
  - Attackers work INDEPENDENTLY and are blind to each other's findings. Redundancy is cheaper
    than a missed case. Merge afterwards.
  - Every case states the DEFECT IT WOULD CATCH, not just the input it sends. A case that would
    pass against broken code is worse than no case, because it reads as coverage.
  - SEVERITY ONE is reserved for: a seat sold twice, a customer charged twice, a customer charged
    and not given a seat, one customer seeing another's booking or payment data, and a
    non-manager reading a cinema's revenue.
  - Test the SILENT failures specifically. A capability that returns an empty result and a 200 is
    indistinguishable from a working one (Art VI.7). Every cross-role case asserts a NON-EMPTY,
    NON-ZERO result for the permitted actor as well as a denial for the forbidden one.

  OUT OF SCOPE: positive cases (GOAL_10), fixing anything found, any application code.

DELIVERABLES - the attack families that must each have a plan and cases
  CONCURRENCY   50 simultaneous holds on one seat . hold and confirm racing . two confirms with
                the same idempotency key at the same instant . confirm after the hold expired .
                confirm a seat someone else now holds . a hold released by a client that does not
                own it . the lock TTL expiring mid-transaction . Redis returning stale state
  MONEY         double submit . refresh mid-payment . payment succeeds and confirm fails .
                confirm succeeds and payment capture fails . a price changed between map render
                and confirm . a negative or zero quantity . an integer overflow on a large booking
  AUTHZ         customer reaching a manager route . MANAGER READING A SCREEN THEY ARE NOT
                ASSIGNED TO (assert BOTH halves - a non-empty, non-zero result for their own
                screen AND a denial for another; a test asserting only "denied" passes against a
                query that returns nothing for anybody) . manager reaching any admin WRITE route
                . a guest reaching anything requiring an account . a role changed mid-session .
                a token for a deactivated account
  DATA          a seat id from another screen . a showtime id from another screen . a booking
                reference belonging to someone else . THE SAME BOOKING REFERENCE PRESENTED TWICE
                at the door (nothing prevents it - SRC-003 OPEN-TICKET-02; the resolution is
                OPEN-DUPREF-01 and is NOT yours to decide) . a booking for a show that has
                already started (legitimate: sales close 15 minutes AFTER the advertised start,
                SRC-003 OPEN-BOOK-03, PROVISIONAL - so this asserts the booking is ACCEPTED)
  DEPENDENCY    Redis down during hold . Redis down during confirm . Postgres down mid-confirm
                . SQS unavailable after a confirmed booking . SQS SLOW OR TIMING OUT INSIDE the
                confirm request . SES FAILING AFTER PAYMENT IS CAPTURED, so the customer has
                paid and has no way into the hall (OPEN-EMAILFAIL-01) . SES ACCEPTING and then
                bouncing, which currently reads as a delivery . the container SIGTERM'd holding
                locks
  ABUSE         the rate limiter keyed on the load balancer rather than the client . a hold loop
                that never confirms, exhausting a screen . poll flooding
