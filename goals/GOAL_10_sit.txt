  Binding rules:
  - ONE case per acceptance criterion. Not one per story, not two per criterion. The mapping is
    1:1 and it is checked mechanically.

  - Every case states its PRECONDITION as data, not prose: which fixture, which seeded rows,
    which clock instant. A case whose precondition is "a showtime exists" is not runnable.
  - NO CASE READS THE WALL CLOCK. Time is injected. A gate that goes red at midnight has told
    you about your test, not your system (Art IX.5, GOTCHAS).
  - Journey cases are SEPARATE from unit-of-behaviour cases and live in their own file, because
    they cross seams and a per-criterion mapping would distort them.

  application code.
