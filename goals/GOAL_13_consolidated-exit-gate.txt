OBJECTIVE
  THE GATE BEFORE CODE. Answer two questions with evidence: do the nine documents agree with
  each other, and can a stranger build from them?

  This layer exists because the cheap mechanical check is blind to the thing that actually
  bites: two documents that each read perfectly well on their own and contradict each other.

CONTEXT - read before starting
  CLAUDE.md . CONSTITUTION.md . GOTCHAS.md
  EVERY document from docs/00 through docs/11. All of them. This goal has no shortcut.

  Binding rules:
  - Reviewers are FRESH ZERO-CONTEXT sessions. A session that watched a document being written
    cannot review it (Art VII.7, Art IX.7). The substitution is recorded.
  - The UNCOACHED SLICE is the load-bearing part: a fresh agent is handed the frozen documents
    and NOTHING ELSE, and asked to build one thin vertical slice. EVERY QUESTION IT ASKS IS A
    DEFECT IN THE DOCUMENTS, not a failing of the agent. Log every question verbatim.
  - A conflict between two APPROVED ADRs cannot be resolved by the build session picking one.
    It is a HUMAN DECISION and goes in the decision queue.
  - The machine verdict is a file, not a sentence in a report. It is either PASS or FAIL and it
    is computed from the counts, not written by hand.

  OUT OF SCOPE: fixing what is found. That is GOAL_13b / docs/13. Writing code.

METHOD - independent lanes, merged
  1. CONFLICT HUNT     every pairwise cross-document contradiction. ADR vs ADR is the highest
                       value pair and the one the mechanical check cannot see.
  2. COVERAGE MATRIX   SRC-001 clause -> BRD row -> PRD spec -> Bible criterion -> SIT case ->
                       ledger row. A break anywhere in that chain is a finding.
  3. UNCOACHED SLICE   the fresh agent builds one vertical slice from the documents alone.
  4. ROOT CAUSE        for every finding, WHY it survived. A finding with no root cause is a
                       symptom and it will come back.
  5. SELF-CHALLENGE    the reviewers attack their own review. What did we not look at? Which
                       claim did we accept without checking? Which document did we skim?

DEFINITION OF DONE - binary
  [ ] 04_COVERAGE_MATRIX.tsv traces every SRC-001 clause through to a ledger row, or names the
      break. Print the break count. A break count of zero on the first attempt is a SUSPICIOUS
      result and needs an explanation of why it is believable.
  [ ] 03_CONFLICT_MAP.tsv lists every cross-document contradiction found, with both sides quoted
      and the authority-order resolution under Art II.1 - or "unresolvable, human decision".
  [ ] 05_UNCOACHED_SLICE_LOG.md records EVERY question the fresh agent asked, verbatim, with the
      document that should have answered it. The question count is the headline number of this
      goal and it is reported worst-first.
  [ ] 02_ROOT_CAUSE_REGISTER.tsv has one row per finding with its root cause. Zero findings
      without one.
  [ ] 06_HUMAN_DECISION_QUEUE.md carries every item the build session may not decide.
  [ ] 07_MACHINE_VERDICT.json is COMPUTED from the counts and reads PASS or FAIL. A FAIL is a
      valid and honest outcome of this goal. Do not adjust a threshold to reach PASS.
  [ ] 08_REVIEWER_SELF_CHALLENGE.md names at least what was not examined and why
  [ ] every reviewer is confirmed zero-context, and the substitution for a second human is
      recorded as a substitution
