## Four things the documents decided, against my own brief

Recorded because in three of them **my first rule would have gone red against a correct schema** —
the same defect class the Bible census spent a day cataloguing.

1. **Primary keys are `bigint GENERATED ALWAYS AS IDENTITY`** (`FR-ADR-017`, which rejects UUID by
   name). My brief told all six readers `uuid` and **all six overrode it**. The ADR's deciding
   argument is this schema's most contended index: two 8-byte integers make a 16-byte key, two
   UUIDs make 32.
2. **`showtime_seat` carries `(showtime_id, seat_id)` too and is NOT the guard.** `P-ADM-14` says
   so in terms. My first rule failed whenever two relations carried the pair. It now checks *which*
   relation guards, and requires every other carrier to declare its job.
3. **`created_at`/`updated_at` DO default to `now()`; nothing else may** (`FR-ADR-018`). My first
   rule banned every clock default and was wrong in the other direction.
4. **Most dotted `table.name` tokens in the Bible are audit actions, not columns** — `movie.published`,
   `screen.deactivated` — and one is the filename `booking.py`. My first version of the highest-value
   rule would have failed ~25 times against a correct schema. The audit vocabulary is now a modelled
   `audit_actions` list (39 members, extracted from `E6`'s published table) checked in both
   directions.

## Two database roles — forced, not stylistic

`frontrow_owner` owns the tables and runs migrations; `frontrow_app` is what the application
connects as.

**Observed on PostgreSQL 16.14, not reasoned:** a table's **owner** sees seven privilege rows
(`DELETE, INSERT, REFERENCES, SELECT, TRIGGER, TRUNCATE, UPDATE`), so `S-XCT-07`'s *"exactly two for
`audit_log`"* **cannot be satisfied by a single role at all**. With a separate owner the same query
returns exactly `INSERT` and `SELECT`. `audit_log` is append-only **because the grants do not
exist**, not by convention.

**No document in this repo contains a `CREATE ROLE`, `GRANT` or `REVOKE`.** The names are a
`[JUDGMENT CALL]` and may change; **the split may not.** Filed as **`AWS_PREP_QUEUE` row 20, needed
by Phase 1** — the advance notice this session owes the client.
