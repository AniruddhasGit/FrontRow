### Pricing rounding is now decided — `FR-ADR-038`

**Integer half-up, per seat, never on the total.** Uplift stored in basis points so the money path
never leaves integer arithmetic.

```
seat_price_paise = (base_price_paise * (10000 + uplift_bp) + 5000) // 10000
booking_total    = sum of already-rounded seat prices
```

Base ₹250 (`25000` paise) at 3333 bp lands on exactly `33332.5` — a true tie — and rounds to
`33333`. Three seats summed after rounding is `99999`; rounding the total once gives `99998`. One
paisa, and the first is the figure the seat map already showed three times.
